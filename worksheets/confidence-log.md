## The honest numbers

- Feel worse around shifts 5–15 than you expected: normal.
- Feel surprisingly capable on routine days by 30–40: common.
- The real "I've got this" turn usually shows up near 60–100.
- If the wheels are still off well past 100: run the four checks in [chapter 6](../docs/06-confidence-math.html) — sleep, feedback, tank (chapter 7), unit fit (chapter 8).
