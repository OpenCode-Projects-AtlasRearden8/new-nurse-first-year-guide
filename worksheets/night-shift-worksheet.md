## After the last night

```
Transition day plan: wake ____ , stay up to ____ , sleep ____ (normal-ish hour)
Errand batch scheduled (daylight): Monday / other: ____
One scheduled real-world social contact this week: ____ (day/time)
```

## Guard rails

- If you catch yourself negotiating with your eyelids on the drive: pull over first, decide second.
- If exhaustion is still compounding after 12 honest weeks on a good schedule, talk to your provider — it's data, not weakness.
